Reading the numbers

Percentages describe the people, product, dose and time window in each study. Symptoms recorded after vaccination can also occur in comparison groups. A relative reduction in risk and the number of events per 100,000 people answer different questions. Each graph labels its measure and links to the original data.

Clinical and family guidance draws first on the American Academy of Pediatrics and the California Department of Public Health. Numerical findings come from original studies, trial results in prescribing information, and disease surveillance. Sources checked November 19, 2026.
