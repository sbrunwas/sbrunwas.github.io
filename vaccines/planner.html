Clinical factors

Check for pregnancy, immunocompromise, asplenia or other high-risk conditions, prior intussusception, severe allergy to a vaccine component, moderate or severe acute illness, recent immune globulin or blood products, travel or outbreak exposure, and prior disease or laboratory evidence of immunity. For HepB, also check birth weight under 2,000 g and positive or unknown maternal hepatitis B status. Use clinician review for foreign records (including oral polio vaccine), nonstandard products or schedules.

Series to plan

Dates cover up to one year. MMR and varicella are planned as separate products. Future HepB doses assume the standard pediatric formulation. Future rotavirus and Hib products are treated as unknown. Seasonal and clinician-review items are handled separately.

How timing is calculated

For children from birth through age 18. Rules checked November 19, 2026. The planner compares the dates you enter with the 2026 AAP schedule and checks the spacing of injectable and intranasal live vaccines. Oral rotavirus uses its own rules. Each result gives the applicable source. Use the clinical review guidance for complex histories and for seasons or products the planner cannot resolve.
